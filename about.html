Learning that connects to real-world skills.

Our mission is to make learning more accessible, relevant and connected to real-world knowledge, skills and opportunities. Navyug Learn is the public face of our learning platform, where people and institutions can discover courses and understand what we offer.

What we focus on

EV and battery technology

Our speciality: electric vehicles, batteries and battery management systems, charging infrastructure, the technologies that support them, and related government initiatives.

More than EV

Navyug Learn is not an EV-only platform. We offer courses in business, technology, design and personal growth, and intend to add more domains.
