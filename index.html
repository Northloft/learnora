Frequently asked questions

What kinds of courses can I take?

Tutorials, Essentials and full Courses. Our speciality is EV and battery technology, including battery management systems and charging infrastructure. We also offer business, technology, design and personal growth courses, and plan to add more domains.

Are all the courses final?

No. Courses marked Sample are demonstration content and are not yet approved Navyug offerings. Prices throughout the site are placeholders.

How does learning work?

Every course is split into short modules and lessons. In this version, progress and notes are kept in your browser as a demonstration of My Learning. The full learning experience will come with our LMS.

Will I get a certificate?

Not yet. We will say clearly which courses include one if and when that changes.

Do I need an account?

You can browse everything without one. The demo sign-in lets you try enrolling and tracking progress, but it is not a real account.

I represent a university, company or government body. Where do I start?

Use the audience links at the top of the page, or the organisations section above, and send us an enquiry.
