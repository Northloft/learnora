Frequently asked questions

What courses can I explore?

Short courses across business, data, computing, design and personal development, with a speciality in EV and battery technology. Browse by subject or search on the Explore page, and open any course to see its outcomes and lessons.

How does learning work?

Each course is split into modules and short lessons. Mark lessons complete, keep notes beside them, and see your progress under My learning.

Are certificates available?

Where a course offers a certification, its details appear on the course page.

How do I access a course?

Open a course from Explore. Free courses can be started right away after you join; paid courses are added to your cart and unlocked at checkout.

Are courses free or paid?

Both. Tutorials are free, and Essentials and Courses are paid. The price is shown on every course card and course page.

Can I learn at my own pace?

Yes. Courses are self-paced, and you can leave and pick up again from where you stopped.
