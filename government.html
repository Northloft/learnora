Questions

Who can enrol?

Public servants, citizens or both, depending on how the programme is set up.

Which languages are available?

Courses are in English today. Tell us which languages you need so we can plan for them.

Do you cover government EV initiatives?

Our EV policy course is a sample. It explains how to read official sources and does not yet summarise specific schemes. We would add those from official publications, with your input, before any programme.

How is learner data handled?

Navyug Learn is in early development. We will agree how data is handled with you before any pilot begins.

Tell us what you need.

Share your department or agency, who the learners are and what you want them to achieve. We will reply by email with options.
